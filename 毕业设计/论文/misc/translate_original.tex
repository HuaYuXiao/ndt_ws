\section{Introduction to Electromagnetic Acoustic Transducers}

Electromagnetic acoustic transducers (EMATs) generate ultrasonic waves directly within conductive materials through electromagnetic induction, eliminating the need for physical coupling agents. When an alternating current flows through a coil placed near a conductive surface, eddy currents are induced within the electromagnetic skin depth of the material. In the presence of a static magnetic field provided by a permanent magnet, these eddy currents experience Lorentz forces, which act as a body force source to generate elastic waves within the material. This transduction mechanism makes EMATs particularly suitable for non-contact and high-temperature testing applications.
