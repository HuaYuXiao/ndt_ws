\begin{englishabstract}
When the UAV is equipped with an electromagnetic ultrasonic probe for structural nondestructive testing, it is difficult to accurately determine the contact state between the probe and the measured surface. The traditional method relies on the amplitude threshold judgment of a single ultrasonic echo signal, which is prone to misjudgment under the conditions of flight disturbance and attitude change. Aiming at this core problem, this paper proposes an autonomous electromagnetic ultrasonic detection framework for UAVs based on multi-modal step-by-step physical constraints, and constructs a time-series perception model that integrates electromagnetic ultrasonic echo sequence, UAV pose dynamic residuals and RGBD local visual ROI features. 
 
At the theoretical level, starting from the electromagnetic-elastic coupling mechanism, this paper regards the EMAT signal as an observable dynamic process formed by the series connection of four physical links : excitation, propagation, reflection and reception, and derives the basic relationship between the Lorentz force as the body load term of the elastodynamic equation. On this basis, the echo statistical characterization modeling of contact boundary and lift effect is carried out. The contact state is defined as a multi-factor hidden variable determined by lift, normal constraint, end mechanical stability and echo observability, and its continuous evolution is expressed in the form of probability. 
 
At the platform and data level, this paper completed the construction and integration of the UAV multi-modal experimental platform. The system uses a multi-rotor UAV as the carrier, equipped with Livox MID-360 lidar, Intel RealSense D435 RGBD camera, PX4-Autopilot flight control and MAVROS communication link, and uses FAST-LIO 2.0 to provide real-time six-degree-of-freedom pose estimation. An EMAT probe ROS driver node based on CH346 C chip is developed, and a stable waveform acquisition of about 40 Hz is realized. A multi-modal synchronous data acquisition system is constructed, which can synchronously record the pose, RGBD video stream and EMAT echo signal, and provide structured data support for model training.

At the algorithm level, aiming at the asynchronous sampling problem of EMAT echo ( about 40 Hz ), flight control odometer ( about 100 Hz ) and RGBD vision ( about 30 Hz ), a weighted interpolation alignment and independent encoder mapping scheme based on unified time reference is proposed. On this basis, a physical constraint attention mechanism is designed, and the time proximity constraint and spatial residual constraint are embedded in Transformer 's scaling dot product attention calculation in the form of logarithmic bias, so that the cross-modal interaction obeys time continuity, spatial proximity and kinematic rationality at the same time. Furthermore, the modal reliability gating weight and the double threshold duration constraint state machine are introduced to realize the complete evidence chain from the sensor uncertainty evaluation to the control closed-loop hysteresis discrimination. 
 
The simulation and experimental results show that the multi-modal physical constraint fusion framework proposed in this paper can effectively overcome the limitations of the single-modal threshold method, and realize the smooth transition of the state discrimination probability in the critical contact section. It provides an integrated solution from physical modeling, signal acquisition, feature fusion to closed-loop control for UAV autonomous electromagnetic ultrasonic testing.
